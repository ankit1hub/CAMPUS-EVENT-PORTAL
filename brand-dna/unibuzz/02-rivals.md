# Rival Map & Category Codes

## Rivals Observed
1. **Luma / Partiful** (Direct Modern Event Tech)
   - *Promise:* Clean, beautiful event invites and instant RSVP.
   - *Visuals Observed:* Minimal white/charcoal canvas (`#000000`, `#FFFFFF`), subtle 1px border lines, heavy typography.
   - *Weakness for Campus:* Not campus-aware; no roll number validation, attendance credits, or faculty verification. `[RESEARCH]`
2. **BookMyShow / Eventbrite** (Commercial Event Platforms)
   - *Promise:* Massive ticketing marketplace.
   - *Visuals Observed:* Loud promotional banners, red/orange branding (`#ED1C24`), heavy upsells and convenience fees.
   - *Weakness for Campus:* Excessive transactional clutter, fees, and unsuited for free campus club workshops. `[RESEARCH]`
3. **University Internal ERP / Blackboard / SAP Portals** (Legacy Campus Systems)
   - *Promise:* Official university compliance.
   - *Visuals Observed:* Dense 2005-era tables, beige/gray backgrounds (`#E0E0E0`, `#333333`), broken mobile layouts.
   - *Weakness for Campus:* Painfully slow, ugly, confusing navigation, hated by students. `[RESEARCH]`
