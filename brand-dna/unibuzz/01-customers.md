# Customer Truth: UNIBuzz

## Jobs To Be Done
- **Functional Job:** Find campus events by date and category, register in under 30 seconds without retyping student info, and carry an offline-ready QR pass. `[RESEARCH]`
- **Emotional Job:** Feel in the loop and confident; eliminate FOMO (fear of missing out on registrations that fill up silently). `[JUDGMENT]`
- **Social Job:** Belong to campus culture, build an extracurricular record with verified certificates, and connect with peers. `[RESEARCH]`
- **Single Biggest Fear:** Missing registration deadlines or showing up to an event only to be turned away because "the Google Form didn't record your entry". `[JUDGMENT]`

## Core Customer Themes
1. **Friction Fatigue with Google Forms & WhatsApp Chaos [High Confidence]**  
   Students report constant frustration with clunky forms that ask for roll number, phone, email, and department every single time.  
   *"Every club uses their own random Google Form. Half the time the link expires or nobody confirms if you're in."* `[RESEARCH]`
2. **Desire for Simple, Noise-Free Discovery [High Confidence]**  
   Students want a clean, honest dashboard of what's happening this week, not 500 unread messages in club group chats.  
   *"I just want to see what hackathons or workshops are happening this Saturday without digging through Instagram flyers."* `[RESEARCH]`
3. **Instant Pass Verification at Campus Gates [Medium Confidence]**  
   Fast QR check-in eliminates long queues outside auditoriums. `[JUDGMENT]`
