# Strategic Positioning & Recommendation

## Position 1: The Modern Campus Signal (Recommended)
- **Position:** For college students tired of WhatsApp chaos and ugly university portals, **UNIBuzz** is the campus event hub that **delivers instant 1-click passes with zero visual noise**, because it unites all club activities under a single, verified editorial interface.
- **Evidence:** Friction fatigue with Google Forms + aesthetic desire for clean, modern tools.
- **Must be true:** Instant QR generation, auto-filled student profile, fast offline-accessible passes.
- **Gives up:** Commercial ticketing, paid third-party event promotions, flashy advertising banners.
- **Personality:** *Tactile, Honest, Effortless.* (Is not: Corporate, Chaotic, Flashy).
- **Colour Direction:** Organic Warm Eggshell (`#F5F1E3`) + Clean White (`#FFFFFF`) with Obsidian Text (`#050505`) and Pacific Teal Accent (`#1B9AAA`).
- **Kill Test:** Can legacy ERP portals copy this? No, their architecture and bureaucracy prevent consumer-grade simplicity. Can Eventbrite copy this? No, Eventbrite cannot authenticate student roll numbers and campus credits.

## Position 2: The Proof-of-Skill Record
- **Position:** For ambitious university students, UNIBuzz is the extracurricular passport that verifies every workshop, hackathon, and club participation.
- **Gives up:** Casual social meetups.
- **Trade-off:** Too narrow; alienates students just looking for concerts and movie nights.
