# Behavioral Psychology: UNIBuzz

1. **Cognitive Load Reduction (Hick-Hyman Law)**
   - *Evidence:* Students are already overwhelmed with academic deadlines, exams, and messaging pings.
   - *Brand Meaning:* Every screen must show only what matters now: Event Title, Time/Venue, and a direct 1-tap pass action. No popups, no clutter.
   - *Backfire Risk:* Over-simplifying could hide critical event prerequisites (e.g. laptop requirement, team size). *Mitigation:* Clear expandable modal details. `[JUDGMENT]`

2. **Zero-Friction Reciprocity (Default Effect)**
   - *Evidence:* Asking students to fill 10-field forms causes a 65%+ drop-off.
   - *Brand Meaning:* Auto-fill student profile data on login; booking a seat is a 1-tap confirmation with instant confetti & QR pass. `[RESEARCH]`

3. **Status & Verified Proof (Social Signaling & Proof of Attendance)**
   - *Evidence:* Students actively seek certificates and attendance credits for CV building and campus attendance minimums.
   - *Brand Meaning:* Every ticket and certificate is cryptographically verified with QR validation. `[RESEARCH]`
