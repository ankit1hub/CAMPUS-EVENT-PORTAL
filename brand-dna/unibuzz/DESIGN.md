# UNIBuzz Design System & Visual Guidelines
*Derived from Position 1: The Modern Campus Signal*

## 1. Palette: Tactile 5-Color Foundation
- **Canvas / Ground:** `#F5F1E3` (Warm Eggshell) - calm, warm editorial foundation that softens screen glare.
- **Surface / Cards:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) - clean contrast, elevates cards with subtle depth.
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#DDDBCB` (Warm Alabaster) - crisp, physical 1px frames instead of heavy blurred shadows.
- **Typography & Ink:** `#050505` (Deep Obsidian) - ultra-legible, high contrast text (Contrast Ratio 15.8:1 > 4.5:1 WCAG AAA).
- **Signal / Accent:** `#1B9AAA` (Pacific Teal) - focused interactive cue for buttons, active tabs, and confirmation passes.

## 2. Typography
- **Typeface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif.
- **Hierarchy:**
  - Page Headings: 2.25rem - 2.5rem, weight 800, tight tracking (`-0.025em`).
  - Section Titles: 1.25rem - 1.5rem, weight 700.
  - Body & Card Titles: 0.95rem - 1.05rem, weight 600.
  - Metadata & Pills: 0.75rem - 0.85rem, weight 500/600.

## 3. UI Rules for "Simple and Clean"
- **Kill the Gradient Soup:** Replace outdated purple/indigo gradient overrides with solid, intentional Pacific Teal (`#1B9AAA`) and crisp white surfaces.
- **Borders over Shadows:** Rely on clean 1px `#DDDBCB` borders and gentle `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)` shadows rather than muddy multi-layer glows.
- **Whitespace Discipline:** Ensure generous breathing room (padding 1.25rem - 1.5rem) inside event cards.
- **Flat, Confident Actions:**
  - Primary button: Solid `#1B9AAA` with `#FFFFFF` text.
  - Secondary button: `#FFFFFF` with 1px `#DDDBCB` border and `#050505` text.
  - Booked state: Soft teal tint `rgba(27, 154, 170, 0.1)` with `#1B9AAA` text.
