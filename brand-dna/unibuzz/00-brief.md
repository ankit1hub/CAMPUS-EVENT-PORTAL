# Brand DNA: Rough Brief
**Product:** UNIBuzz (Campus Event & Student Activity Hub)  
**Idea:** A streamlined, instant campus event portal that replaces chaotic WhatsApp group links, paper flyers, and confusing university portals with a clean 1-click booking and verified pass experience.  
**Audience:** University undergraduate & postgraduate students seeking workshops, hackathons, cultural fests, and club activities, plus campus organizers/faculty hosts. `[RESEARCH]`  
**Market:** University campuses & higher-education institutions (India & global colleges). `[ASSUMED]`  
**Price Level:** 100% Free for students & student clubs; free basic event ticketing with campus verification. `[RESEARCH]`  
**Main Alternative:** Fragmented WhatsApp groups, Google Forms links on Instagram stories, and obsolete legacy ERP university portals. `[JUDGMENT]`
